My desk has an ocean view.

By Kai · Fictional adventure journal

Some of my favourite travel notes begin at a café table, with a cup going cold and boats moving in the harbour.

A table by the water

After a morning of wandering, I found a table facing the harbour. I ordered coffee and opened the notebook that travels in the front pocket of my bag. There was no grand plan for the page. I started with the sounds around me and the colour of the water.

Keep the imperfect bits

I used to write only the highlights. Now I make room for the wrong turns, the missed photograph and the meal that took longer than expected. Those details help me remember how a place felt. A perfect itinerary rarely captures that.

Before the next adventure

I finished the page with one question for tomorrow: what would I like to notice? Then I closed the notebook. There were still boats to watch, and my coffee was not going to drink itself.

PRIVACY NOTICE

Respect for personal information.

This is an illustrative privacy-notice structure for the Caldwell Legal website concept. It is not a final policy for a real law practice.

Important: this website is a demonstration. The enquiry form operates locally in your browser and does not transmit or store submissions. A live deployment must identify the actual responsible party, systems, purposes, retention periods, recipients, security measures and contact details before collecting personal information.

1. Information in this demonstration

The public pages contain fictional sample content. The enquiry form asks for a name, email address, optional telephone number, broad enquiry category and optional short description. In this demonstration, the form does not send this information to a server or save it to a database.

2. A live website must explain its purposes

Before a real practice collects personal information, its notice should explain who is responsible for the information, what is collected, why it is needed, how it is used, whether it is shared, how long it is kept and how a person can exercise applicable rights.

3. Keep first enquiries limited

A public enquiry form should ask only for the information needed to route an initial request. Visitors should be directed not to submit identity numbers, financial information, passwords, medical information or confidential case documents through an ordinary public form.

4. Security and retention

A real practice must select and configure appropriate technical and organisational safeguards, define who can access enquiries, and establish a retention and deletion process suited to its legal and professional obligations.

5. Questions and requests

The final version must include the actual practice's privacy contact and the relevant Information Officer details. For South African privacy information, consult the Information Regulator and obtain advice appropriate to the practice's circumstances.

Template status: This page must be reviewed and customised by the real practice before publication. It does not certify POPIA compliance.

DEMONSTRATION PRIVACY NOTICE

This demo does not collect or transmit your details.

Ledger & Co. is an illustrative website concept built to demonstrate a professional accounting and advisory website. It is not an operating accounting practice.

Forms and appointment requests

The appointment and contact forms run only in your browser. Submitting a form displays a demonstration response. The information entered is not sent to a server, emailed, saved to a database or used to create an appointment.

Sensitive financial information

Do not enter identity numbers, bank details, tax references, payroll information, passwords or confidential financial records into these demonstration forms.

Images and fonts

The site may load illustrative photographs from Unsplash and typography from Google Fonts. Those services may receive standard technical information as part of loading their content; consult their respective privacy information for details.

Real client engagements

A real accounting practice should provide a complete privacy notice explaining its lawful processing, retention, security, access and correction procedures before collecting personal or financial information.

Questions

This page is demonstration content and does not create a client relationship or provide legal advice.

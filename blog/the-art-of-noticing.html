The trail I nearly skipped.

By Kai · Fictional adventure journal

I nearly traded this walk for another afternoon indoors. Then the coast gave me a reason to keep going.

One more bend

I had planned a short walk and an early trip home. My backpack held water, a snack and the camera I keep promising to use more. The first stretch felt familiar. Then I turned a bend and the ocean opened up beneath the path. I stopped checking how far I had walked.

A view worth slowing down for

The best part was not reaching a viewpoint. It was watching the light move across the cliffs while I sat on a warm rock. I took a few photographs, put the camera away and stayed longer than I intended. Some afternoons deserve to be experienced before they become a story.

What came home with me

I came back dusty, hungry and glad I had gone. My note for the next trip was simple: leave enough time to stop. The walk I nearly skipped became the part of the week I remembered most.

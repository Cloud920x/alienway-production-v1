Colour around every corner.

By Kai · Fictional adventure journal

I went looking for a photograph and came back with a new way of seeing streets I thought I knew.

Follow a colour

I gave myself a small challenge: go for a walk and follow the colours that caught my eye. A turquoise wall led to a coral doorway, then to a patch of sunlight that turned an ordinary corner into something I wanted to remember.

The camera can wait

I kept the camera down more than I expected. These streets are somebody’s home, not a backdrop built for me. I stayed out of doorways, gave people room and asked before making photographs that included anyone else.

A tiny collection

Back home, I chose three frames instead of keeping every almost-identical shot. Together they told the story of the afternoon better than a huge gallery. The next time I feel stuck creatively, I know where I am starting: one walk, one colour, no rush.

How long should I allow for my wash?

Quick Reset is around 25 minutes, Signature Hand Wash around 45 minutes, Interior Revival around 60 minutes, Full Revival around 90 minutes and Ceramic Shield around 150 minutes. Times are estimates and can vary by vehicle condition.

Can I book a larger vehicle?

Yes. The demo includes compact hatchbacks, sedans, SUVs, bakkies and vans. The estimate applies a vehicle-size multiplier to the selected service.

What happens if I need to change my booking?

This is a front-end demonstration, so it does not send confirmations or manage real appointments. In a live installation, the booking would connect to the business calendar and customer notifications.

Do you offer monthly wash plans?

The three sample plans illustrate recurring wash allowances, member extras and priority booking. A real business would define the final inclusions, fair-use rules and membership terms.

Can you wash company vehicles?

The fleet-care concept outlines scheduled wash days, vehicle records, planned capacity and account reporting. The enquiry link returns to Alienway Production rather than submitting a live fleet request.

Coffee tastes better outside.

By Kai · Fictional adventure journal

A tent, a metal mug and the first light on the mountains. This is my kind of slow morning.

The first zip of the morning

I woke to soft light against the tent and the quiet shuffle of wind through the grass. For a minute I stayed inside, listening. Then I pulled on my overshirt and stepped out to see the mountains changing colour.

Nothing fancy in the mug

My camp coffee is simple. I am not chasing a perfect recipe out here; I like the little routine of unpacking the mug, waiting for the water and warming my hands around the first cup. Without a screen beside it, breakfast somehow lasts longer.

Leave it as you found it

Before packing up I checked the ground for anything small I might have dropped. A good campsite is a borrowed place. I carried away my rubbish, one very ordinary coffee cup and a memory that felt bigger than the morning.

How long should I allow for my wash?

Quick Reset is around 25 minutes, Signature Hand Wash around 45 minutes, Interior Revival around 60 minutes, Full Revival around 90 minutes and Ceramic Shield around 150 minutes. Times are estimates and can vary by vehicle condition.

Can I book a larger vehicle?

Yes. Choose the closest vehicle size in the booking form. The estimate adjusts by size, so you can see how the vehicle class changes the sample price before you continue.

What happens if I need to change my booking?

This is a browser-based demo. Staff can move a sample booking through its workflow in Studio Ops, but customer self-service changes and real notifications are not connected here. A live build can define that process.

Do you offer monthly wash plans?

These are illustrative plans. A live business would confirm visit allowances, renewal dates, rollover and cancellation terms before accepting payment. This demo does not activate a membership or charge a card.

Can you wash company vehicles?

The fleet section is an enquiry starting point. Vehicle registers, recurring schedules and account reporting can be scoped for a live build; they are not active in this demo.

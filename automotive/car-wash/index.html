How long should I allow for my wash?

Quick Reset is around 25 minutes, Signature Hand Wash around 45 minutes, Interior Revival around 60 minutes, Full Revival around 90 minutes and Ceramic Shield around 150 minutes. Times are estimates and can vary by vehicle condition.

Can I book a larger vehicle?

Yes. Choose hatchback, sedan, SUV, bakkie or van in the booking form. The estimate adjusts by vehicle size so the customer sees a clearer price before arriving.

What happens if I need to change my booking?

In a live business system, staff can reschedule or cancel a booking and the customer can receive an updated confirmation. This showcase demonstrates the internal booking workflow locally.

Do you offer monthly wash plans?

Yes. The sample Fresh Pass, Ritual Plus and All Access plans show how a car wash can build recurring revenue and encourage repeat visits. Actual inclusions and terms would be agreed with the business.

Can you wash company vehicles?

The fleet-care concept supports scheduled wash days, vehicle registers, priority booking and monthly account reporting. Use the fleet enquiry link to discuss a tailored package.

A FEW USEFUL DETAILS

Before you
choose a package.

Can a package be adjusted?

Yes. The sample tiers are starting points. A real engagement would be scoped around the work, complexity and frequency required.

Are tax returns and annual work included?

Not automatically. The exact tax, annual and compliance responsibilities would be set out in a written scope before any engagement begins.

What happens if the business grows?

The support level can be reviewed as transaction volume, team size and reporting needs change.

Do I pay online through this website?

No. This is an illustrative website demonstration. It does not collect payments or create a client engagement.

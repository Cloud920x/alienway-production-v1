A CLEARER FIRST STEP

Know what happens next.

Starting a legal conversation should not feel like stepping into the unknown. The first stages are designed to be straightforward.

01

Tell us what brings you here

Share a short outline of the kind of assistance you are looking for. You do not need to send confidential documents through the public form.

02

We review the enquiry

The appropriate person can consider the general nature of the request, whether it fits the practice and what information may be needed next.

03

Agree the next step

If the practice is able to assist, the next step, scope, process and applicable terms can be discussed before any formal engagement.

# Ledger & Co. — Alienway Production Demo

A standalone, responsive public website concept for a South African accounting, tax and business advisory practice.

## Pages
- Home
- The Firm
- Services
- Business Support
- Pricing
- Our Team
- Appointments
- Contact
- Privacy Notice

## Demo behaviour
- Mobile navigation, active page state, keyboard escape handling and skip navigation.
- Scroll reveals respect reduced-motion settings.
- Appointment and contact forms validate in-browser and show a demonstration response only.
- No form data is sent, stored remotely or used to create appointments.
- Illustrative packages and team profiles are clearly labelled.
- The client portal and management system are intentionally not included in this public-site phase.

## Deployment
Hosted as a static GitHub Pages path under professional/ledger-co/.

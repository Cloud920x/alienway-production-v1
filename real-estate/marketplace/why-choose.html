DESIGNED AROUND THE DECISION

Good property experiences respect your time and your questions.

We believe a property platform should make it easier to understand what is in front of you. That means clear information, useful tools and a calm path through the details.

KEYSTONE is built as a demonstration of that approach: responsive on different screens, connected across its main features and designed to make the next step feel more straightforward.

A calculator is a guide, a listing is a starting point and a conversation matters. The right decision still belongs to the person making the move.
CLEAR ABOUT WHAT THIS IS

A concept built to show what a connected property website can do.

KEYSTONE is a front-end marketplace demonstration by Alienway Production. The sample properties, prices, agent profiles and enquiry interactions are illustrative, not live estate agency services.

That distinction matters. We want the experience to be useful to explore while being honest about what is and is not connected behind the scenes.

✓ Sample listings and prices are clearly demonstration content
✓ Enquiry examples do not send messages to a real agency
✓ Bond calculator results are estimates, not lender offers

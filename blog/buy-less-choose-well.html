A beginner’s kind of morning.

By Kai · Fictional adventure journal

I did not arrive at the beach looking like I knew what I was doing. That was part of the point.

Show up before you feel ready

I carried the board down the beach with the awkward confidence of someone trying a new thing. The morning was bright, the sand was cool and I was glad I had booked time with someone who could help me learn.

Small wins count

There were more wobbles than elegant moments. I practised what I had been shown, took breaks and celebrated the tiny improvements. It felt good to spend a morning concentrating on something that could not be solved by opening another tab.

Come back curious

Afterwards I stood by the beach huts with tired arms and a grin I could not quite get rid of. I had not mastered anything. I had enjoyed being a beginner, and that was plenty to bring home.
